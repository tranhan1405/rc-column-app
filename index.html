1Deformation capacities

Chord rotation (drift) at the equivalent yield point, at peak force and at a 20 % drop in lateral strength. Tool values are medians with 16–84 % bounds from the out-of-sample error, and design values exceeded by 95 % and 90 % of tests (from design data, or anchored on the tests entered).

ASCE/SEI 41-17 Table 10-8 (point C = θy + a, a median value per the ACI 369.1M-17 Commentary) and EN 1998-3 Eq. (A.1) (γel = 1; divide by 1.5 for primary elements) are computed from the same inputs. Validation leaving out whole test programmes (299 tests, 64 programmes): tool σln 0.44–0.61 without bias (tests without 20 % strength loss as lower bounds), but θ80 overestimated by 23 % for lightly confined, shear-critical columns of the ACI 369 set; ASCE 41-17 σln 0.78 at 20 % strength loss (PEER); for flexure-controlled PEER columns the measured drift at 20 % strength loss was 0.85 of the EN 1998-3 value (median), and 35 % of the tests fell below θum/1.5. Design factor on the median at 20 % strength loss: 0.37 (5 %) and 0.48 (10 %) from design data; one test of the same column type raises it by about 30 %.

2Collapse fragility

Probability of reaching peak strength (LS1) and a 20 % strength loss (LS80, the near-collapse state of EN 1998-3) for the single-degree-of-freedom system of period T and mass m. Lognormal in Sa(T); β combines record-to-record, prediction and strength uncertainty.

Basis: incremental dynamic analysis with the 44 FEMA P695 far-field records on 299 calibrated Pinching4 models (about 10 million analyses). Out-of-sample error of the median collapse intensity at LS80: σln 0.23–0.33 with no systematic bias, against σln 0.26–0.63 and a median overestimate of 8–70 % for backbones from ASCE 41-17 or Haselton et al. (2016) analysed in the same way.

3Pinching4 model for OpenSees

Lateral force–displacement spring (kN, mm) at the column end: backbone from the predicted capacities and strength, cyclic parameters as medians of the 245 calibrated PEER columns of the same shear class.


        
Replayed under the 299 test histories (out of sample), this model gave a median response error J of 0.33, against 0.44–0.46 with the ASCE 41-17 backbone and the same cyclic parameters, and 0.28 with the measured backbone. It was better than the ASCE 41-17 model for 81–85 % of the columns.
